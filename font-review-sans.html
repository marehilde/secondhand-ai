Ten sans-serif combinations, weighted toward urgency

Same format, all sans this time. Each card renders the wordmark, a section heading, body, and a research band with a stat in a candidate heading font. Three flavours of sans urgency: newsroom grotesque (broadsheet authority), humanist / contemporary (clear and current), and condensed / poster (breaking-news volume).

Body copy is held to Public Sans in every card so you're comparing the heading font. Colours are the final Ambient Haze palette. Fonts load from Google — give it a second on first open.

Colours

  • Amber and dust lead, with teal accents, over calm neutral grounds
  • Amber: urgency without alarm
  • Teal accents: direct attention, convey secondhand AI has positive and negative consequences
  • The gradient builds urgency as you scroll, mirroring how effects accumulate

You've heard about the effect of AI on the people who use it. This is about everyone else.

What is secondhand AI?

Take a moment to think about what you'd guess it means.

Secondhand AI — the downstream effect of one person's AI use on what others receive, how they interpret it, and what they pass on.

It's about how AI reaches into our social interactions — shaping what we say to each other, and how we say it. Let's look at an example.

Secondhand AI · visual design · for review

Design overview

Direction: Ambient Haze — teal accent, amber & dust warmth.

Secondhand AI has effects that are two-sided (they can help and harm), often invisible and hard to spot, and carry real urgency. The palette expresses this without alarm: a clean neutral "clear air" that a warm amber haze gradually creeps across (the urgency, building rather than shouting), teal as the quiet recurring accent for the beneficial side, and colour kept restrained so the writing leads.

The existing page layouts are unchanged — this covers only how colour is applied across them.

The palette

One family: a neutral ground, a single dark, teal for accents, and amber/dust for warmth.

Real life examples

Neutral ground, colour in the words

The gallery sits on the plain clear-air ground (#E9ECEA), keeping it calm and neutral. Colour lives in the type instead — teal source tags, with amber for the sharper cases — so the page stays quiet while the words carry the accent.

Research paper pages

Each study is built from exactly three colours — one dark, one light tint, one whiteish neutral — as full-width bands (Title · Summary · Procedure · Results · Key findings · Implications), text directly on the colour. The band order is identical across all four; only the three colours change, so a paper can carry a cooler or warmer identity while the pages stay a consistent family.

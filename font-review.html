Ten font combinations, weighted toward urgency

Each card renders a real page context — the wordmark, a section heading, body copy, and a research band with a stat — in a candidate heading font. They span four ways type can carry urgency: editorial serif (gravitas), newsroom sans (broadsheet authority), condensed / poster (breaking-news volume), and slab (documentary weight).

Body copy is held to Public Sans in every card so you're comparing the heading font on equal footing. Colours are the final Ambient Haze palette. Numbers 09 (Spectral, current) is included for reference. Fonts load from Google, so give it a second on first open.

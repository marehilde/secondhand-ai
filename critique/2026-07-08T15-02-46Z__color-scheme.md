## Overall Impression

The color *system* is well-conceived and, where it's followed, executes cleanly — one constant gold accent, a legible chapter-color-block structure, a genuinely clean Parchment/dark-surface text-triad separation. The problem is consistency of execution: Research Detail.dc.html breaks the system's central rule outright (zero gold anywhere on the page), and every page has accumulated undocumented near-token colors that a linter or a second designer would immediately flag as drift. Layered on top of that are real accessibility failures — most severely, fixed navigation chrome that goes almost invisible the instant a user scrolls onto the Parchment sections of Research Detail (contrast ratios of 1.36:1 and 1.95:1), and the site's one intentional warning callout being effectively unreadable at ~1.44:1.

## What's Working

1. **Zero gradient-clipped text or hero-metric styling anywhere in the four live files** (verified by direct search) — the anti-SaaS commitment in PRODUCT.md genuinely holds at the code level.
2. **The Parchment text triad is applied cleanly and exclusively** wherever Parchment appears — Espresso Ink heading at 11.35:1, Warm Umber body at 5.9–7.4:1 — a real, working execution of the Parchment Boundary Rule.
3. **Every gold-background interactive element is rock solid**: all gold-button/tab text pairings (`#241F18` on `#E6D4AC`) land between 9.2 and 11.2:1 — the interaction pattern users touch most often is both legible and calm.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Fixed nav chrome unreadable over Parchment sections**
- **Why it matters**: On Research Detail.dc.html, the white contents-rail (`#FFFFFF` on `#E8DCC4` = 1.36:1) and the "← Back" link (`#F4EEE0` on flattened `~#B5AC99` = 1.95:1) are hardcoded for dark dusk backgrounds. The moment a reader scrolls into Procedure or Implications, navigation effectively disappears — a genuine usability failure, not just a soft accessibility miss.
- **Fix**: Make chrome color reactive to the same `data-section` state the component already tracks in its scroll logic — swap to `#2B2410` text / darker backdrop when the active section is Parchment-backed.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`

**[P0] "The One Gold Rule" broken across an entire page**
- **Why it matters**: `#E6D4AC` appears zero times in Research Detail.dc.html (confirmed by search). Its primary CTA uses an undocumented white/`#4A3C73` pairing instead of the documented gold `button-primary` spec, and ~12 other undocumented colors (`#C9BCE8`, `#9FC0E8`, `#DBE6F2`, `#F2F6FC`, `#4A3C73`, etc.) fill the rest of the page. This is the system's single named constant failing on a whole surface, not a one-off drift.
- **Fix**: Restore the documented `button-primary` spec (`#E6D4AC` bg / `#241F18` text) for "Read the full paper," and add at least one more gold touchpoint (e.g. recolor the Procedure step-number circles from `#8A7A54` to gold) so the page rejoins the rest of the site.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`

**[P1] Muted Gold / Dusty Taupe fail contrast as small text against bright gradient stops**
- **Why it matters**: Concrete failures — hero eyebrow `#CDBA92` on the gradient's blue top stop = 2.80:1; mode-row captions `#B6A98D` = 2.80:1 (×3, repeated pattern); stat-chip text `#E6D4AC` on its own chip fill = 3.94:1; inactive research-tab text = 3.25–3.34:1. This is a systemic pattern (the same two colors failing repeatedly), not isolated incidents.
- **Fix**: Either darken the translucent card/chip fill (`rgba(255,255,255,.05–.08)` → `~.16`) to increase effective contrast, or swap Muted Gold/Dusty Taupe for Pale Gold `#F4EEE0` on anything under 18px against bright gradient stops.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`

**[P1] ~44 undocumented off-palette colors accumulating across the site**
- **Why it matters**: The detector confirms 50 color values outside DESIGN.md's frontmatter, 44 of which have no documented justification (6 are legitimate — they match prose-documented shadows the detector can't parse). Two of these are also outright contrast failures used for meaning: `#8FB8B1` (3.34:1) and `#C98A63` (2.52:1) in the "deep vs. shallow" comparison. Left unaddressed, this pattern will keep growing every time a new page is added.
- **Fix**: Sweep each undocumented color against the nearest DESIGN.md token and replace, or — where a genuinely new need exists (e.g. the deep/shallow comparison) — swap to existing tokens (Warm Ivory `#ECE3D0` for both, reinforcing "you can't tell them apart") rather than inventing new hues.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`

**[P2] The system's one intentional warning color is its least legible element**
- **Why it matters**: The "⚠ vague error" chip computes to ≈1.44:1 (`#B4532A` text on `rgba(180,83,42,.18)` fill) — the single deliberate alarm note in the entire site is currently unreadable, undercutting the moment it exists to serve.
- **Fix**: Raise the chip fill opacity to ~.35–.4, or lighten the text toward Peach Coral `#F0A98A`.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`

You've heard about the effect of AI on the people who use it. This is about everyone else.

What is secondhand AI?

Secondhand AI — the downstream effect of one person's AI use on what others receive, how they interpret it, and what they pass on.

Three modes

One story, three ways it reaches Bob

1
Content overload

AI makes it cheap for Alice to produce, costly for Bob to verify.

2
Illusory expertise

AI can mask whether Alice's understanding is deep or shallow.

3
An altered sender

Using AI changes Alice's own perspective — and how she talks to Bob.

Why it matters

We judge each other by what we produce. When AI changes what fluency means, it quietly changes how we read everyone — including the people who never touched it.
